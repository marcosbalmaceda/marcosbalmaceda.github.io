Hi! I'm Marcos, a PhD candidate in Business Economics at KU Leuven, in the Management, Strategy, and Innovation (MSI) department. In January 2027 I will join Universidad del Desarrollo as an Assistant Professor in Entrepreneurship.

I study how firms and entrepreneurs respond to generative AI. My research asks why entrepreneurs adopt AI slowly even when they believe it pays, how AI reshapes credibility and governance inside organizations, and when algorithms can substitute for human advisors. It sits at the intersection of entrepreneurship, strategy, and organizational economics, and combines formal models with field experiments and large-scale observational data.

My work on mandatory AI disclosure is under revision at the Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal and won the 2026 G-Forum Norbert Szyperski TIM Research Award. My work on technology licensing and inventor mobility was nominated for both the 2026 SMS Annual Conference PhD Paper Prize and the SMS Strategic Human Capital Interest Group Best PhD Paper Award. I co-design randomized trials on AI mentorship with France Travail (90,000 job seekers) and with schools in Chile, Peru, and Ecuador (120,000 students). I have presented this work at the Conference on Field Experiments in Strategy, the Advances with Field Experiments Conference at the University of Chicago, the SMS Annual Conference, DRUID, and the Academy of Management. I'm a research network member of the Innovation Growth Lab (IGL).
